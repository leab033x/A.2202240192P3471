The GREAT Scottish Rent Trap: Is There a Future for Young Scots?

For young people under the age of 30 in Scotland, the promise of independence and financial security after university is increasingly overshadowed by a constant state of uncertainty. As property prices and rental costs continue to outpace wage growth, an entire generation is finding themselves at a breaking point. According to the Scottish Government’s research, housing affordability and the rising cost of living have emerged as the primary concerns for young people looking toward the next two decades.

This trend isn't just a temporary struggle in the market, the crisis runs deeper. It is a structural issue in our society that is breeding hardship and inequality making them a permanent part of life in Scotland. For many students finishing their degrees this year, the choice is starting to look pretty bleak: stay here and struggle to keep our heads above water, or leave the country to find a place where they can actually afford to live.
