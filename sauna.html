The Radical Rise of Scottish Sauna Culture

Scotland has spent decades trying to shy away from its climate. But in recent year, the strategy has changed: we’re no longer hiding from the cold—we’re using it to fuel a multi-million pound wellness boom.

THE DIGITAL UPTICK

The data doesn’t lie: search volume for “Outdoor Saunas Scotland” reached an all-time high in 2025. This surge in searches reflects a broader shift in attitudes towards wellness and slow living, particularly in the wake of the pandemic. As interest in wild swimming and eco-tourism continues to grow, outdoor saunas sit at the heart of health, leisure, and aesthetic experience. With demand peaking especially during colder months the trend signals not only changing consumer behaviour but also a clear opportunity within Scotland’s wellness economy is heating up.
